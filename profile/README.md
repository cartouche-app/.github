**On iPhone and iPad**, Cartouche installs, starts and keeps the games [nxnative](https://github.com/seedds/nxnative) runs natively, from your own copies, and signs, renews and updates itself with a free Apple account.<br>
**On the Mac**, [Cartouche Builder](https://cartouche-app.github.io/Cartouche/#builder) builds your games, plays them there with the same saves as your iPhone, and builds them again when nxnative improves them.

**No games included, ever.** Cartouche ships no games, no keys and no firmware, and never downloads them: you bring your own copies, dumped from your own console. Please don't ask for IPAs.

### Source code

**[cartouche-app/Cartouche](https://github.com/cartouche-app/Cartouche)** holds all of it: the app for iPhone and iPad, Cartouche Builder for the Mac, the Cartouche Installer for Mac and Windows, and the website. It is free software under the AGPL-3.0, and issues and pull requests are welcome ([contributing](https://github.com/cartouche-app/Cartouche/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)).
